# Hovering utility drone visual concept

The owner supplied this as another robot design idea. Its defining form is a compact oval body with a large central blue optic, secondary lenses, small manipulators and illuminated underside propulsion details.

Reference image: [reference.jpg](reference.jpg), preserved unchanged. The printed name and descriptions of sentience do not define the project's name or controller architecture.

## Features to preserve

- A prominent forward lens cluster that gives the drone an expressive face.
- An oval armored shell with small fins, vents and a distinctive underside assembly.
- Off-white plating, a dark inner structure, blue optical and propulsion accents, and amber indicators.
- Compact tool arms and a hovering posture with readable banking and waiting poses.

## Adaptation across graphics packs

Follow the [shared graphics pack plan](../graphics-quality-tiers-v0.1.0.md). Low can use a faceted shell, a clear central eye and simple manipulators and propulsion cues. High and optional Ultra can add layered optics, articulated fingers, recessed vents and richer mechanical surfaces while keeping the same robot identity and physical interaction positions.

This is an appearance concept for the proposed flying-drone family. Flight, navigation, energy use and supported tools require their own gameplay design and verification.
